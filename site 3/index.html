Notes

Projection. Source rasters in EPSG:32612 (UTM 12N) at 30 m resolution; reprojected to Web Mercator for display. Rendered with perceptually appropriate colormaps — diverging schemes are centered at zero, density is displayed on a log scale, probability on a linear 0–1 scale.

Forest mask. When "Forest only" is on, every layer is restricted to NLCD classes 41 (deciduous), 42 (evergreen) and 43 (mixed). The aspen density and high-regen layers are forest-only by construction; the toggle is most informative for phenology and burn severity, which otherwise span the full study extent.

Reading the map. Each layer's opacity is independently adjustable. Hover the map to read out latitude and longitude. Click + drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

Field of view
Monroe Mountain, Sevier County, Utah — a sub-alpine plateau in the Fishlake National Forest known for some of the largest contiguous aspen stands in the Intermountain West.
